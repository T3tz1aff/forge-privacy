FORGE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

FORGE (“the app”) is a strength-training app. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: your training data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account. The developer cannot see it, and it is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Who we are

FORGE is developed by Alexander Senf (“we”, “us”). Contact: kontakt@senf-spricht.de.

What data the app handles

FORGE works with the data you enter or grant access to, including:

  • Training data — your plans, workouts, sets, reps, weights, and personal records.
  • Body metrics — body weight and any measurements you choose to log.
  • Health & recovery metrics — heart rate, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, and sleep, only if you grant Apple Health access. These are used on your device to calculate your daily readiness score and adjust your training load.

Where your data is stored

On your device, and in your own private iCloud account (Apple’s CloudKit private database), so it syncs across your devices.

We do not operate our own servers for your training data and have no access to your private iCloud database. We cannot read, retrieve, or recover your data.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, FORGE reads heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, and sleep to gauge recovery, and writes your completed workouts so they appear in Apple Health and Apple Fitness. HealthKit data stays on your device / in Apple Health. It is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never shared with third parties or transmitted to us.

Third-party connections (optional, only if you enable them)

  • Strava — if you connect Strava, FORGE uploads your completed workouts to your Strava account at your request, using Strava’s official OAuth. This happens only when you initiate it. Data you send to Strava is governed by Strava’s Privacy Policy.
  • Garmin / other recovery sources — if you import recovery data, that exchange is governed by the respective provider’s privacy policy.

You can disconnect these services at any time in the app’s settings.

What we do NOT do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device and in your private iCloud. To delete it: delete the app to remove on-device data, and/or remove the app’s data from iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

FORGE is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
